# Help set Go's priorities for next year

## Краткое содержание

The text is a brief announcement directed at the Go programming language community. It invites developers to participate in the 2026 Go Developer Survey. The survey aims to collect personal experiences and insights from Go developers. Participation is voluntary and the call is open to anyone working with or interested in Go. The survey window is currently active, with a stated deadline of November 3. The message is straightforward and promotional in nature, serving as a reminder or prompt for engagement. Overall, it is a single-sentence call to action within the Go ecosystem.

## Полная статья

## [The Go Blog](https://go.dev/blog/)

Hi Gophers! The[2026 Go Developer Survey](https://blink.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ba8fa1ZcSQQdTRs?s=b)is now open. We run this survey every year to understand how you use Go and the challenges you face. Your answers directly shape how we plan and prioritize our work.

Last year, 5,379 of you responded, and your top frustration was making sure your code follows best practices and Go idioms (33% of respondents). Since then, we shipped a[rewritten`go fix`](https://go.dev/blog/gofix)in Go 1.26 that automatically modernizes your code, and added more modernizers in Go 1.27. You can read everything we learned in the[2025 results](https://go.dev/blog/survey2025).

[Take the survey here](https://blink.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_ba8fa1ZcSQQdTRs?s=b). It’s open through**November 3**, takes about 10–20 minutes, and every question is optional. We’ll share the results on this blog in early 2027.

Please share the survey with your colleagues, friends, and online communities—the more Go developers we hear from, the better we understand what you need.

Thank you for helping shape the future of Go!
